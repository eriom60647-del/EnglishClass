The Origin of Chinese New Year

A class reading about Nian and the traditions of Chinese New Year
This page is ready for your full class script. The text below is a short sample version for layout and testing.

Long Ago...

Long ago, there was a monster named Nian. Nian lived near a village and came out every year. The villagers were afraid of the monster because it was big, strong, and frightening.

One day, the villagers discovered that Nian was afraid of three things: the color red, bright fire, and loud noises. They decided to use these things to protect their homes.

The Plan

The villagers put red paper on their doors, lit lanterns, and made loud sounds. When Nian came to the village, it saw the red decorations and heard the loud noises. Nian became frightened and ran away.

A New Tradition

From then on, people celebrated the new year with red decorations, lanterns, firecrackers, family gatherings, and good wishes. These traditions became part of the Chinese New Year celebration.
