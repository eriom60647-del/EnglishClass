SCENE 2

The Village Chief’s House

Time: Afternoon
Place: Hippo Village
👑
Village Chief
Oh! Everyone’s back!
👥
All Villagers
Thank you, Chief!
🐰
Villager A
Have you heard the story? They say there’s a monster living in the mountains. Every year, it comes down and attacks the villages. The Giraffe Village next to us has already been destroyed!
🐱
Villager C
Relax, relax! It’s just a story. There’s nothing to be afraid of.
🐻
Villager B
No, it’s true! When I went shopping, I saw huge claw marks all over the village. And everyone was gone!
SCENE 3

The Village Chief’s House

Time: Night
📖
Narrator
To celebrate the great harvest, the villagers gather together for a big dinner.
👑
Village Chief
Thank you, everyone, for all your hard work. Dinner is on me tonight!
👥
All Villagers
Hooray!
📖
Narrator
But halfway through the celebration, suddenly, the wind begins to blow and the ground starts to shake!
🎬
Action
The Nian Monster enters with loud BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! sounds.
🐲
Nian Monster
Hello, everyone! I’m the Nian Monster! My name is Xi. I’m so hungry! I haven’t eaten anything for a whole year! Tonight, I’m going to have a BIG feast!
🎬
Action
The Nian Monster starts destroying things.
📖
Narrator
Everyone screams and runs away!
📖
Narrator
That night, Hippo Village suffers terrible damage. The whole village is left in ruins.
SCENE 4

The Village Chief’s House

Time: Daytime
📖
Narrator
The next day, the villagers gather together to discuss how they can defeat the Nian Monster.
👑
Village Chief
Cheer up, everyone! Everything has a weakness. If we can find Xi’s weakness, we can defeat him!
👥
Villagers
Yesterday, we saw him trying to stay away from anything red!
🐱
Villager C
And something exploded yesterday. It really scared him!
🐰
Villager A
I have a kitchen knife and a cutting board at home. We can use them to make a really loud noise!
🎬
Action
Chop! Chop! Chop!
👑
Village Chief
Great! Then we’ll use these two things to defeat the Nian Monster!
